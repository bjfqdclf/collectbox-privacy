## FAQ

**1. How does sync across devices work?**
With the same Apple account and iCloud enabled, your collection syncs via iCloud. Changes upload when the app goes to background; on the other device, pull to refresh or tap "Sync iCloud data" for an immediate check. Without an iCloud account the app uses local storage; sign in and relaunch to restore sync.

**2. How do I back up or move my data?**
Settings → Export saves a JSON backup via the system file picker. On a new device, Import that file to restore everything, including photos.

**3. How do preorder deposits and balances work?**
When adding an item, switch to Preorder mode and enter any two of deposit / final payment / total; the third is calculated automatically. Pending balances and overdue arrivals are highlighted on the Home and Preorder tabs.

**4. Which devices are supported?**
iPhone and iPad on iOS/iPadOS 18.0 or later. On iPad the list uses an adaptive card grid with popup detail and edit windows.

**5. How do I change the app language?**
Settings → Language: Simplified Chinese, English, or Japanese, applied instantly. "Follow System" matches your device language.

**6. Is my data safe?**
Records and photos live only on your device and in your own private iCloud space; the developer cannot access them. See the [Privacy Policy](https://bjfqdclf.github.io/collectbox-privacy/en.html).
