# CollectBox Privacy Policy

**Effective date: November 19, 2026**

CollectBox ("the app") is developed and maintained by independent developer Anhua Lei. This policy explains how the app handles your information. **In short: the app collects no personal data.**

## 1. Data we do not collect

The app has no user accounts, registration, or login (your iCloud account is managed by the system, not by the app). We therefore do not collect:

- Identity information such as name, email, or phone number
- Location data
- Contacts, photo metadata, or device identifiers
- Usage analytics or crash statistics (no analytics or advertising SDKs are integrated)
- Any tracking data (no cookies, no cross-app tracking)

## 2. Where your collection data lives

Records you create (names, prices, purchase dates, channels, photos, preorder and balance information) are stored only:

1. **On your device**, in the app's local database;
2. **In your own iCloud space**, when sync is enabled, via Apple CloudKit's **private database**, syncing across your devices.

Data in a CloudKit private database is accessible **only by you** through your Apple account. **The developer cannot read, access, or back up any of your collection data.**

## 3. Import and export

Backup export and import happen entirely on your device: you choose where exported files go and which files to import. Nothing passes through any server; the developer neither participates in nor can obtain these files.

## 4. Third-party services

The app contains no third-party SDKs, ad networks, or analytics tools. The only external service involved is **Apple iCloud / CloudKit**, used to sync your own data, governed by [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## 5. Children's privacy

The app collects no data from anyone, including children. Its content is suitable for all ages.

## 6. Data security

Because data never leaves your device and your own iCloud space, the app itself stores nothing on developer servers and transmits nothing to them. On-device and iCloud data are protected by iOS security mechanisms and Apple's encrypted transport and storage respectively.

## 7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will note it via app update notes or by updating the effective date on this page. Continued use of an updated version constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
